About

A passionate academician with over 20 years of rich experience in teaching, research, and academic leadership, currently serving as an Associate Professor in the Department of CSE(AI&ML) at PES University, EC Campus, Bangalore. My core research interests lie in Cybersecurity, Blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), Machine Learning applications in Security, Wireless Sensor Networks, Adhoc Networks, and Data Science. I have made significant scholarly contributions with 18 research publications in reputed refereed journals and 30 papers presented at national and international conferences.
Throughout my career, I have demonstrated a strong commitment to academic excellence by guiding more than 60 undergraduate project batches and over 15 postgraduate research projects. Actively organized Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), technical workshops, and seminars on emerging technologies and has been a sought-after guest speaker and seminar resource person in various institutions. An active member of leading professional bodies including IEEE, ACM, IE(India), and ISTE. Known for passion for mentoring and research-driven teaching, continues to inspire students and contribute to advancing the frontiers of computing and intelligent systems.
