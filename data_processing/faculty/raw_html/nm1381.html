About

Deepti.C is currently working as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science Engineering, PES University EC Campus, Bengaluru, India. She has graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Visvesvaraya Technological University in 2004.She obtained her M. Tech in the field of Computer Network Engineering from Visveswaraya Technological University in the year 2009. She is currently pursuing PhD in Computer Science Engineering at Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU). She has 18+ years of teaching and research experience in academics. She is a life member of Computer Society of India (CSI). Her area of interest includes Computer Networks, Internet of Things and Wireless Sensor Networks. She is a lifetime member of CSI. She is an active researcher in the field of Computer Networks and Internet of Things. Her research interests include Wireless Sensor Networks, Computer Networks, Internet of Things (IoT) and Machine Learning.
