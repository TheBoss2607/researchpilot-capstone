About

Dr. Arti is Professor and Chairperson of CSE(AI & ML), EC Campus. She completed her B.Sc. (Hons) in Mathematics in 1994 and M.Sc. in Mathematics in 1996 from Delhi University. She later earned her M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Allahabad Agricultural University and received her Ph.D. in Computer Science Engineering from the Faculty of Technology and Engineering at Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak, Haryana, in 2009.

She served as a Professor and Head of the MCA Department at PESIT, Bangalore South Campus, until August 2020. Since then, she has been a Professor in the Department of Computer Science Engineering at PES University. With over 25 years of academic experience, including 16 years dedicated to research, Dr. Arya’s expertise spans Spatial Data Mining, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Graph Neural Networks, Generative AI, Unstructured Data Management, Applied Numerical Methods, and Biostatistics.

She is a Senior Member of IEEE, a life member of CSI and IAENG. Additionally, she serves on the reviewer board of several reputed international journals, including SNAM (Springer, Scopus indexed) and IJCDS (Scopus indexed). Dr. Arya has approximately 95+ publications in various esteemed international conferences and journals. She is currently guiding 2 PhD scholars and 2 have already completed in the area of natural language processing and spatial data mining. She has supervised approximately 200 undergraduate students for various research projects. Further details can be found on her LinkedIn profile https:// in.linkein.com/in/artiarya.
